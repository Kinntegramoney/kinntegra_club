<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The initial request was to build a cashflow calculator for Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs). The calculator needed to handle bond details (name, dates, principal, and interest schedules) and determine the price for a secondary buyer to maintain a fixed IRR. The primary buyer would act as a broker, earning the spread.

The project evolved into a full-stack B2B platform with the following PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:

    *   **Admin Module**: Accessible only to Brokers. Used to Add Bond and Create/Edit/Delete Sub-broker.
    *   **Opportunities Module**: Displays bonds in three sections based on their status: Available, Funded, and Closed.
    *   **Dashboard**: A general overview page.
3.  **Bond Management (Broker)**:
    *   Brokers can create, edit, and delete bonds.
    *   A bond's status changes:  (when created) ->  (when all units are sold) ->  (when all repayments are complete).
4.  **Secondary Market Calculator**:
    *   Sub-brokers can view bond details and use a calculator.
    *   The user inputs an approximate investment amount, and the calculator shows the upper bound and lower bound of units they can buy.
    *   The final price is calculated based on the selected unit count.
5.  **Cashflow & Reporting**:
    *   Ability to download a month-wise cashflow report in CSV format.
    *   The report must include principal payments, gross interest, TDS (calculated at 10%), and the net amount.
    *   The cashflow amounts in the report should be scaled based on the number of units chosen by the secondary buyer.
    *   The CSV should be correctly formatted (INR numbers, yyyy-mm-dd dates) without encoding artifacts.
6.  **IRR Calculation**:
    *   The secondary IRR is a **gross** IRR. The purchase price for the secondary buyer is calculated using **gross** cash flows to meet this target IRR.
