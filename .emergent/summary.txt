<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The initial request was to build a cashflow calculator for Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs). The project evolved into a full-stack B2B platform for brokers and sub-brokers to manage secondary market transactions.

PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:
1.  **User Roles & Authentication**: Two roles (, ) with a two-factor authentication system (PAN, then Password + PIN).
2.  **Platform Structure**: A dashboard, an Opportunities section to view bonds (categorized as Available, Funded, Closed), and an Admin section for brokers to manage bonds, sub-brokers, and clients.
3.  **Bond Management**: Brokers can create, edit, and delete bonds. A bond's status changes based on its sales lifecycle.
4.  **Secondary Market Calculator**: Allows users to calculate the purchase price of bond units based on a target IRR. It shows price bounds for an approximate investment amount.
5.  **Client & Trade Workflow**:
    *   Brokers can manage a list of clients, including creating them with document uploads (PAN, Aadhaar).
    *   Brokers or Sub-brokers can book bond units for a client from the  page.
    *   The booking process requires uploading proof of payment.
    *   All trades initiated by sub-brokers must be approved by a broker via a Trade Verification page. Units are only deducted from availability after approval.
6.  **Business Logic & Rules**:
    *   For fully funded bonds, the calculator and delete functionality must be disabled.
    *   The system must prevent booking more units than are available.
    *   Clients and sub-brokers cannot be permanently deleted; they are marked as inactive (soft delete).
7.  **Reporting**: Ability to download a month-wise cashflow report in CSV format, scaled to the number of units purchased.
8.  **UI/UX**: The application must be mobile-responsive.

**User's preferred language**: English
