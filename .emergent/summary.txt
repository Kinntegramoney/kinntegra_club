<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The initial request was to build a cashflow calculator for Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs). The project evolved into a full-stack B2B platform for brokers and sub-brokers to manage secondary market transactions.

PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:
1.  **User Roles & Authentication**: Two roles (, ) with a two-factor authentication system (PAN, then Password + PIN).
2.  **Platform Structure**: A dashboard, an Opportunities section to view bonds, a Trade Verification page, a Holdings module, and an Admin section for brokers to manage bonds, sub-brokers, and clients.
3.  **Bond Management**: Brokers can create, edit, and delete bonds. A bond's status changes based on its lifecycle (, , ).
4.  **Secondary Market Calculator**: Allows users to calculate the purchase price of bond units based on a target IRR.
5.  **Client & Trade Workflow**:
    *   Brokers manage clients, including document uploads (PAN, Aadhaar).
    *   Users can book bond units for a client, requiring payment proof.
    *   Trades initiated by sub-brokers must be approved by a broker.
6.  **Holdings Module**:
    *   A new Holdings page in the sidebar.
    *   Shows a client's investments, repayments (upcoming, repaid), and projected cashflows.
    *   Trades for the same bond are consolidated.
    *   A modal with tabbed views: one for a consolidated cashflow summary by date, and separate tabs for each individual transaction's cashflow.
    *   Allows brokers/sub-brokers to manually mark cashflows as Repaid.
    *   A Profile tab to display the selected client's KYC information.
    *   Functionality to download holdings data as a multi-sheet Excel file.
7.  **Reinvestment Tagging**:
    *   A new tab within the Trade Verification page.
    *   It should have Tagged and Untagged sections.
    *   Allows tagging of upcoming repayments for future reinvestment, with a Not Invest option.
    *   Entries are grouped by client.
8.  **UI/UX**: The application must be mobile-responsive.

**User's preferred language**: English
